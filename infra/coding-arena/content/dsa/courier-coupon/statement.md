A courier has to get a parcel from junction **1** to junction **n** of a city. The city has **m** two-way roads; road i joins junctions uᵢ and vᵢ and charges a toll of **wᵢ** rupees each time it's used.

The courier also has **one coupon** that makes a single road toll-free, once. The coupon doesn't have to be used.

Print the smallest total toll to get from 1 to n, or **-1** if n can't be reached.
