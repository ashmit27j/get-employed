Ankit is tracking the price of a Tatkal train ticket for **n** days. On day i the price is pᵢ rupees.

For each day i, let the **wait** wᵢ be the number of days from day i until the first later day whose price is **strictly higher** than pᵢ. If no later day is higher, wᵢ = 0.

Print the sum w₁ + w₂ + … + wₙ.
