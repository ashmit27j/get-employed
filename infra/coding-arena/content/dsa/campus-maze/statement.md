The campus map is a grid of **R** rows and **C** columns. Each cell is open (`.`), a wall (`#`), your hostel (`S`) or the exam hall gate (`E`). There is exactly one `S` and one `E`, and both are open.

In one move you can step to the cell above, below, left or right, as long as it is open and inside the grid.

Print the fewest moves needed to get from S to E, or **-1** if E can't be reached.
