A delivery drone always flies with **exactly two parcels**, and it only takes off when their combined weight is exactly **K** grams. Any lighter or heavier and the balance is off.

The warehouse has **n** parcels in a row, with weights a₁, a₂, …, aₙ. Weights can be negative: the warehouse records counterweights as negative values.

Count how many ways there are to choose two different parcels **i < j** such that aᵢ + aⱼ = K.
