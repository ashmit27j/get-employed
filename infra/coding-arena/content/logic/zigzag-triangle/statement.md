Print a triangle of numbers with **n** rows. Row i contains **i** numbers. The numbers 1, 2, 3, … are written in order, row after row, but the direction alternates:

- odd rows (1st, 3rd, …) are written **left to right**,
- even rows (2nd, 4th, …) are written **right to left**.

Separate the numbers in a row with single spaces.
