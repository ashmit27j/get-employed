An exam hall has **R** rows of **C** seats, and every seat has a roll number written on it. The invigilator collects answer sheets in a **clockwise spiral**: starting at the top-left seat, right along the top row, down the right side, left along the bottom row, up the left side, and then inwards in the same way until every seat is visited.

Print the roll numbers in the order the sheets are collected.
