In the Gregorian calendar, a year is a **leap year** if it is divisible by 4, **except** that years divisible by 100 are not, **except** that years divisible by 400 are.

A long-term planning tool asks **q** questions. For each pair **L, R**, print how many leap years there are from year L to year R, inclusive. Apply the rule to every year, however far in the future.
